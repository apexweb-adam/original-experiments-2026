Which AI offer can you actually deliver?

Decision brief · 12 September 2026 · original synthesis from the supplied source study and current checks. Prepared as a sample research deliverable; no client commission or payment.

Decision

Test a bounded deliverable with an identifiable buyer before buying distribution or building a subscription business. A paid-test request is stronger evidence of present demand than a creator’s monthly-income headline, but it still needs identity, scope and payment verification.

1. Motion service

The motion-service source offers a concrete production workflow, while its income/cost claims remain creator reports. A September 10 public request seeks vector/isometric motion and proposes a small paid test. The buyer asks for actual work, rate and tools. A local original sample meets only the production step; compatibility and commercial acceptance remain open.

2. Spreadsheet service

The dashboard source demonstrates an interface and manual refresh. It does not prove data reconciliation, privacy or customer acceptance. Google already provides a Sheets connector, so a custom interface needs a specific advantage: validated calculations, a simpler decision or a safer workflow.

3. Local visibility

The SEO playbook can inform an evidence-bound service. Its income target is not a forecast. Google describes several ranking factors; a category change is not a guaranteed causal win.

4. Trading claims

Dozens of supplied sources repeat wallet, payout or backtest gains. The reviewed material does not establish attributable, cost-aware forward profitability. The transferable work is research quality: preserve rejected ideas, test fills and fees, and keep execution separate. No live financial experiment is selected.

What would change the decision?

  • A buyer accepts a specific deliverable and paid terms.
  • Measured delivery time or revisions make the price uneconomic.
  • A user can solve the same problem with an adequate free substitute.
  • An integration or distribution channel is unavailable.

Limits

175 available video captions, 11 Higgsfield article texts and 35 X texts were studied; six videos were unavailable. Most visuals and all X-attached videos remain unreviewed. Source income claims are not independently audited. These are ranked test hypotheses, not ten validated businesses.
