Revenue is only the first number.

A short original lesson for people evaluating online income claims. No copied footage, automated voice or financial recommendation.

1. Start with a complete transaction

A screenshot of sales says little about attribution or take-home money. Record what was sold, whether the payment settled, refunds, transaction fees, delivery cost and the time required. Keep customer details private.

2. Count the work

A 300-unit order with a 15% fee and 30 units of production cost leaves 225 before labor, tax and overhead. At eight hours, that is 28.13 per hour before those remaining costs. A second revision changes the economics.

3. Test the next uncertain step

A finished sample tests production. A person using it tests usefulness. A paid agreement tests willingness to pay. A settled payment tests collection. None automatically proves repeatable acquisition or profit.
