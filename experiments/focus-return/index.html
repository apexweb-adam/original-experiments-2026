      A small return ritual

      1. Make the next action visible. “Work on the report” leaves a decision to make. “Write three rough bullets” gives you a place to start.
      2. Put the easiest distraction a little further away. Move your phone or close the feed tab if you can. This page cannot enforce that for you.
      3. Capture the interruption. Park a passing thought. For a real interruption, pause and leave a return note before switching.
      4. Come back to the note. Resume with that single action. If your plan no longer fits, change it.
      5. Finish deliberately. Note what remains. A timed window ending is an invitation to check in, not a reason to interrupt useful work.

      An original practice aid, not a clinical treatment or a promise of productivity gains. Try one session and decide whether it helps your work.
